# Terms of use (draft)

Draft for the pilot. Not legal advice; have it reviewed before a public launch.

1. **What the app is.** A free guide to Mumbai trains, metro and last-mile options. It is not run by Indian Railways, MMRDA, MMRTA, any bus operator, or Uber, Ola or Rapido, and is not endorsed by them.
2. **Information can be wrong.** Times come from official timetables when entered, fares are estimates from published tariffs, and commuter reports come from other users. Check official sources for anything important. The meter or the provider's app decides the real fare.
3. **No emergency use.** Don't rely on the app in an emergency. Call 112.
4. **Ride apps.** Booking buttons only open the provider's own app. Your booking, price and trip are between you and that provider.
5. **Community reports.** Report only what you saw. No abuse, personal information about others, or advertising. Moderators may remove reports and disable accounts that break these rules.
6. **Your account.** You can delete it at any time from Profile. See the privacy notice for what that removes.
7. **Map data.** © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence.
8. **Changes.** These terms may change; the app will show the new version.
