# Privacy notice (draft)

Version 2026-10-v1. The same text is in the app (Profile → Privacy notice) in English, Marathi and Hindi. This is a plain-language draft, not legal advice; have it reviewed before a public launch.

**What we keep.** If you sign in: your email and name from your sign-in, your language, saved routes, the reports you send, when your account was created and the last day you used it. Without an account, nothing about you is kept on our server.

**What we never keep.** Your password (Google Firebase handles sign-in), your location history, contacts or phone number. There are no ads, no tracking and we never sell data.

**Location.** Your phone's location is read only when you tap "My location" and stays on the phone. When you choose a destination in the last-mile finder while signed in, the station and destination points are sent to our server to look up the road distance.

**Reports you send.** Other people see the report, its place (to about 10 metres) and its time, never your name or account. Moderators can see which account sent a report, only to deal with abuse. Reports disappear when they expire.

**Your choices.** Delete account (Profile) removes your email, name and saved routes and closes your sign-in. Reports you sent stay for other commuters but are no longer linked to you. You can use the app without an account.

**Services we rely on.** Google Firebase (sign-in) and Google Apps Script and Sheets (our server and storage); OpenStreetMap tile servers when the map loads; openrouteservice for road distances if enabled; Photon (komoot), which searches OpenStreetMap data, receiving only the words you type into place search; and Uber, Ola or Rapido only when you tap their button.
